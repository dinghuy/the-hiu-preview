# The Hiu — internal website prototype

## Brief
- User: “phù hợp với thị hiếu khách nước ngoài”; no single international aesthetic is assumed. Working direction: restrained, easy-to-read hospitality editorial.
- User: “Một hành trình hình ảnh liền mạch khi cuộn”. Use one continuous visual field with layered imagery and restrained motion; photographic transitions are a prototype, not a continuous filmed tour.
- User: “Chưa chọn villa, làm bản mẫu nội bộ trước”. Photos from Photos/9A LLQ are internal working material only; selection and publication rights pending.
- Audience: international visitors, with English copy. Purpose: lead enquiry for one whole villa. Rates, capacity, property facts, contact and channels await verification.
- Feeling curve: arrive (calm); discover (curiosity); picture the stay (warmth); plan (clarity). Peak: the living space revealed inside the visual field. Close: a legible enquiry preview.
- Peak sentence: “It's the site where the villa gradually comes into view as I plan a stay.”
- Vibe, signature move and individual section sequence are provisional design choices; no further user statement is implied.
- Signature: an overlapping photograph moves at a separate rate over the garden view, suggesting entry into the house. Quiet holds are intentional when reading.
- Inputs: local garden and interior photos. No synthetic depiction, pricing or unsupported property facts.
- CTA: Check your dates. Preview only; no real submissions, no collection or external connection.

## Score and scope
The desired continuous-world direction differs from chaptered, rail, index, dossier, catalogue, split-screen and conventional editorial layouts. This internal still-photo prototype approximates it with one pinned photo field and flowing content; matched video footage needed for a true seamless worldflight is unavailable. Four beats: layered entry; overlapping room reveal; transparent information preview; enquiry. The empty fingerprint registry has no existing rows to compare.
